**The classic experiment:** Schrödinger's Cat

You've probably heard: Put a cat in a box with a quantum device that might kill it. According to quantum mechanics, until you open the box and look, the cat is **both alive and dead at the same time**.

**The question that's haunted physics for 100 years:** Why does looking at it make it "choose" to be one or the other?

5. You see one definite result

**Why consciousness matters:**

Only systems with high integrated information (Φ) can "collapse" quantum states. Why? Because consciousness is what **integrates information** - it connects different pieces together into a unified whole.

**This means:**
- Looking at something = High-Φ system (you) interacting with quantum system

### Dark Matter = Hidden Information

Remember the holographic principle? Information is encoded on boundaries?

**Here's the key:** That information can be encoded in different "bases" - different ways of organizing it.

**Analogy:** 
- You can describe your location as "10 meters north, 5 meters east"
- OR as "11.2 meters at 63.4° angle"
- **Same location, different encoding**

**For the universe:**

**Why 85%?**

The math of holographic encoding shows that information naturally splits ~15% observable, ~85% orthogonal. **Exactly what we see!**

### Dark Energy = Information Cost

**The puzzle:** Why is the universe expanding, and why so slowly?

**Our answer:** There's a "cost" to encoding all the universe's information on its cosmic boundary.

**The math:**
```
Maximum information = Area of cosmic horizon / 4 (in Planck units)
Cost per bit = Tiny amount of energy
Total cost = "Dark energy" pushing space apart
```
