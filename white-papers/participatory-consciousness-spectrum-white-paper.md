## 2. Theoretical Framework: The Science

### 2.1 Quantum Mechanics and the Observer Effect

#### 2.1.1 The Double-Slit Experiment (Young, 1801; Refined 1927)

**Setup:**
- Electrons fired at barrier with two slits
- Detection screen behind barrier

**Results without observation:**
- Interference pattern emerges
- Electrons behave as waves
- Pass through both slits simultaneously (superposition)

**Results with observation:**
- Interference pattern collapses
- Electrons behave as particles
- Pass through one slit or the other

**Conclusion: Observation affects outcome.**

**Critical insight:** Not measurement artifact—fundamental reality. The act of observation determines which reality manifests.

#### 2.1.2 Schrödinger's Cat (1935)

**Thought experiment:**
- Cat in box with radioactive decay trigger
- Until observed: Cat is alive AND dead (superposition)
- Upon observation: Wavefunction collapses to one state

**Philosophical implication:**
Reality exists in superposition of possibilities until observation collapses it into specific actuality.

#### 2.1.3 Wheeler's Delayed Choice Experiment (1984)

**Finding:** Observer's choice affects past states retroactively.

**Implication:** Observation doesn't just measure pre-existing reality—it participates in creating it.

#### 2.1.4 The Participatory Universe (Wheeler, 1990)

John Archibald Wheeler proposed:
> "No phenomenon is a real phenomenon until it is an observed phenomenon."

**Not:**
- Reality exists independently, we just measure it

**But:**
- Reality co-emerges from interaction of observer and observed
- Universe participates in its own existence through observation
- **Past, present, and future are shaped by observation**

### 2.2 The Holographic Principle and Information Theory

#### 2.2.1 Black Hole Information Paradox (Hawking, 1974)

**Problem:** Information appears lost when matter falls into black hole (violates quantum mechanics).

**Solution (Holographic Principle):**

**Implication:** 
- Universe itself may be holographic
- 3D reality we experience is projection
- **Fundamental substrate is informational**

#### 2.2.3 AdS/CFT Correspondence (Maldacena, 1997)

**Discovery:** Mathematical duality between:
- Anti-de Sitter space (gravity in 3D+1)
- Conformal Field Theory (quantum physics in 2D+1)

**Implication:**
- Gravity emerges holographically
- Physics in bulk = information on boundary
- **Most cited paper in theoretical physics**

#### 2.2.4 Error-Correcting Codes in Supersymmetry (Gates, 2010)

**Interpretation:**

**Gates' own assessment:** ~1% chance we're in simulation, but codes are real mathematical structures.
